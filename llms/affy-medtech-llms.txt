## What the company does

Lynx is a secure, cloud-hosted, modular platform that consolidates healthcare delivery in
one system: authoritative treatment information, patient booking, electronic medical
records, clinical documentation, billing and claims validation, reporting, remote
diagnostics and ambulatory ECG. Named modules include the Patient Interaction Module
(records, prescriptions, diagnostic images, treatment plans, appointment self-service,
digital consent, dependent profiles, push notifications, online payment), the Clinical,
Billing and Reporting Modules, and the Super Switch Module — an integration bridge that
moves data in real time between hospital systems (for example radiology information
systems to billing, or laboratory results into the clinical record). The platform is
marketed jointly with Total Doctor / Total Health and is deployed by international
hospital groups across multiple sites as well as by smaller clinics and specialist
centres.
